# Contractor and Intern Policy

This policy applies to contractors, freelancers and interns working with Lumen Labs. Where it differs from the Employee Handbook, this policy takes precedence for these groups.

## Working hours

Contractors agree their hours with the hiring manager in their statement of work. Interns follow the same core collaboration hours as employees but may not work more than 40 hours per week, and never between 22:00 and 07:00.

## Time off

Contractors are not entitled to paid annual leave; days on which no work is done are simply not invoiced. Contractors should still announce planned absences of more than two days to their manager at least one week in advance so that work can be planned.

Interns on contracts of three months or longer receive 2 days of paid leave per month of internship. Unused intern leave is paid out at the end of the internship and cannot be carried over to a later contract.

## Sick days

Contractors do not receive paid sick leave. Interns receive up to 5 paid sick days per internship. Interns must inform their mentor before 10:00 on each day of absence; a medical certificate is required from the second consecutive day.

## Remote work

Contractors may work fully remotely unless their statement of work says otherwise. Interns are expected in the office at least four days per week during their first month, so that they can learn from their team, and at least three days per week afterwards. Interns do not receive a home office stipend.

## Equipment

Contractors normally use their own computers, which must meet the requirements of the IT security policy: full-disk encryption, a supported operating system, and the company device-management profile installed before accessing any internal system. Interns receive a company laptop on their first day, which is returned on their last day.

## Invoicing and payment

Contractors submit invoices monthly through the supplier portal by the 5th day of the following month. Invoices are paid within 30 days of approval. Expenses are only reimbursed if they were approved in writing in advance and are listed on the invoice with receipts attached. Contractors must submit expense receipts within 45 days, after which they are not reimbursed.

## Learning and conferences

Contractors do not have a learning budget. Interns can request up to 300 USD for books or online courses related to their internship project, with approval from their mentor.

## Confidentiality and intellectual property

All contractors and interns sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving access to any internal system. Work produced under a statement of work or an internship agreement belongs to Lumen Labs. Contractors must not reuse code written for Lumen Labs in work for other clients.

## Access

Contractor accounts expire automatically on the end date in the statement of work. Extensions must be requested by the hiring manager at least five working days before expiry. Contractors never receive production write access; if a task requires it, an employee must perform the change.

## Ending an engagement

Either side may end a contractor engagement with the notice period stated in the statement of work, which is two weeks if nothing else is agreed. Internships end on the date in the internship agreement; early termination requires one week of notice from either side.
