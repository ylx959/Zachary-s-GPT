# Atlas X1 Robot Vacuum — User Manual

The Atlas X1 is Lumen Labs' entry-level robot vacuum for apartments and small homes.

## Specifications

- Suction power: 2,500 Pa
- Battery: 3,200 mAh lithium-ion
- Runtime: up to 110 minutes on the quiet setting
- Charging time: about 4 hours from empty
- Dustbin capacity: 450 ml
- Water tank: none (the X1 does not mop)
- Navigation: gyroscope plus bump sensors
- Weight: 2.9 kg
- Height: 7.6 cm, low enough to fit under most sofas
- Noise level: 58 dB on the quiet setting

## Setting up

Place the charging dock against a wall with at least 0.5 m of free space on each side and 1.5 m in front of it. Charge the Atlas X1 fully before the first run. Pair it with the Lumen Home app by holding the Home button for 3 seconds until the light blinks blue. The X1 only supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks.

## Cleaning modes

The X1 offers three suction levels: Quiet, Standard and Max. Spot mode cleans a 1 m by 1 m square around the robot. Because the X1 navigates without a map, it cleans in a random-bounce pattern and cannot be told to avoid specific rooms; use the included 2 m magnetic boundary strip to block off areas.

## Maintenance

Empty the dustbin after every run. Wash the filter every two weeks and replace it every 3 months. Replace the side brush every 6 months and the main roller brush every 12 months. Clean the cliff sensors with a dry cloth once a month.

## Troubleshooting

- Error E1 (wheel stuck): lift the robot and check both drive wheels for hair or debris.
- Error E2 (side brush jammed): remove the side brush with a screwdriver and clean it.
- Error E5 (battery temperature): move the robot to a room between 10 and 40 °C and wait 30 minutes.
- If the robot does not return to the dock, make sure the dock is not in direct sunlight, which interferes with its infrared beacon.
- To factory reset the X1, hold the Home and Spot buttons together for 10 seconds.

## Warranty

The Atlas X1 comes with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The battery is covered for 6 months. Damage caused by liquids is not covered, because the X1 is not designed for wet surfaces.

## Package contents

The Atlas X1 box contains the robot, the charging dock, the power adapter, two side brushes (one pre-installed), one spare filter, a cleaning tool with a built-in blade for cutting hair from the roller, the 2 m magnetic strip described above, and a quick start guide. Keep the box for the first 30 days in case you want to return the robot.

## Indicator lights

- Solid white: the robot is fully charged and ready.
- Pulsing white: charging.
- Blinking blue: waiting for Wi-Fi pairing.
- Solid blue: connected to the Lumen Home app.
- Blinking red: an error has occurred; check the app or the error list below.
- Solid orange: battery below 15%; the robot is returning to the dock.

## Scheduling

Schedules can be set in the Lumen Home app or directly on the robot. To set a daily schedule without the app, hold the Spot button for 5 seconds at the time you want the robot to start every day; the robot beeps twice to confirm. Only one schedule can be stored on the robot itself, while the app supports up to seven schedules, one per weekday.

## Floor types

The X1 works on hard floors and on low-pile carpets with a pile height of up to 1.5 cm. It climbs thresholds of up to 1.6 cm. On thick rugs and high-pile carpets, the robot may get stuck; use the magnetic strip to keep it off them. The automatic carpet boost of the X2 Pro is not available on the X1, so select Max suction manually when you want deeper carpet cleaning.

## Battery care

To extend battery life, keep the robot on its dock when it is not in use and avoid leaving it fully discharged for longer than a week. If you will not use the robot for more than a month, charge it to around 50%, switch it off with the power switch underneath, and store it in a cool, dry place. A healthy X1 battery keeps about 80% of its original capacity after 400 full charge cycles.

## Replacement parts

Genuine replacement filters, side brushes and roller brushes can be ordered in the Lumen Home app under "Shop". A maintenance kit with three filters, four side brushes and one roller brush costs 29 USD. Using third-party parts does not void the warranty, but damage caused by unsuitable parts is not covered.

## Safety instructions

Do not use the X1 outdoors, on wet floors or near open flames. Remove cables, socks and small objects from the floor before cleaning, because the X1 has no obstacle camera and will try to drive over them. Keep the robot away from stairs without a railing if the cliff sensors are dirty. Children should not sit or stand on the robot. Only use the original power adapter supplied in the box.

## Technical support

If the troubleshooting steps above do not solve the problem, contact support through the Lumen Home app. Have the serial number ready; it is printed on the label under the dustbin and starts with "X1-". The firmware of the X1 updates automatically over Wi-Fi at night while the robot is on its dock.
