# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

Lumen Labs is a fictional robotics company used as an evaluation corpus. This handbook covers the policies every employee should know in their first month.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local office time zone. Outside core hours employees may arrange their day freely, as long as they attend scheduled meetings. Teams that support customers in other regions agree their own overlap windows with their manager.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31. Leave requests of three days or more must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is only required when an absence lasts longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over and cannot be exchanged for pay.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are anchor days on which everyone in a hybrid team is expected in the office. Fully remote arrangements need approval from both the department head and HR, and are reviewed every six months.

Employees working from home receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 USD, paid with the first salary after the request is approved. The stipend can be used for a chair, desk, monitor or headset. Receipts must be kept for two years in case of audit.

## Expenses and reimbursement

Business expenses are claimed through the Expensify app. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date; claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Approved claims are paid out with the next payroll run, which happens on the 25th of each month.

Economy class is the default for flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than eight hours, and requires prior approval from a director.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference attendance that requires travel also counts against the travel budget of the team, not only the learning budget.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from two peers chosen by the employee, and a manager assessment. Salary adjustments are decided once a year after the December cycle and take effect on February 1.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of three months. During probation, either side can end the contract with one week of notice. After probation the notice period is one month for individual contributors and two months for managers.

## Equipment

Every engineer receives a laptop on their first day, chosen from the standard catalogue. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours (see the IT security policy).

## Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee's contract is held. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is taken off instead. Employees who are asked to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day that must be used within 60 days; it cannot be paid out.

In addition to public holidays, the whole company closes for a "recharge week" between Christmas and New Year. Recharge week does not count against annual leave. Teams that run customer-facing services keep a skeleton crew during that week and the people on duty get the same number of days off in January.

## Overtime

Individual contributors are not expected to work more than 40 hours per week on a regular basis. When a manager asks for weekend work, the employee receives time off in lieu at a rate of one and a half hours for every hour worked. Time off in lieu must be agreed in advance and recorded in the HR portal by the end of the month in which the work happened.

## Business travel

All business travel is booked through the company travel desk, which can be reached through the travel channel in chat. Hotels are reimbursed up to 220 USD per night in major cities and up to 150 USD per night elsewhere. Employees who prefer to stay with friends or family receive a flat 40 USD per night instead.

Trains are preferred over flights for journeys shorter than four hours door to door. Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed for travel to and from airports and train stations, and after 21:00 for safety reasons. Personal car use is reimbursed at 0.45 USD per kilometre; parking and tolls are claimed separately.

Travel insurance is provided automatically for every business trip. Employees travelling to countries with an elevated travel advisory need written approval from the security team at least ten working days before departure.

## Health and wellbeing

All full-time employees are covered by the company health insurance plan from their first day. Spouses and children can be added to the plan at no extra cost. Every employee also receives an annual wellbeing allowance of 300 USD that can be spent on gym memberships, sports equipment, therapy sessions or meditation apps.

The employee assistance programme offers free and confidential counselling, available 24 hours a day by phone. Up to eight counselling sessions per issue are covered in full. Managers never learn who has used the programme.

## Referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and passes probation receive a referral bonus of 2,000 USD. For hard-to-fill engineering roles marked as "priority" on the careers page, the bonus is 3,500 USD. Referral bonuses are paid with the salary of the month after the new hire's probation ends. Recruiters, hiring managers and members of the interview panel for the role are not eligible.

## Internal mobility

Employees may apply for internal roles after 12 months in their current position. The current manager must be informed before the first interview, but cannot block the move. Successful internal candidates normally move to the new team within six weeks so that handover work can be completed.

## Code of conduct

Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than 50 USD must be declared to the compliance team and may need to be returned. Employees must not accept invitations to events that include paid travel or overnight stays from current or prospective suppliers. Conflicts of interest, such as a family member working for a supplier, must be disclosed in the compliance portal as soon as they arise.

Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are never tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline run by an external provider. All reports are investigated within 30 days.

## Leaving the company

Resignations must be submitted in writing through the HR portal. Unused annual leave is paid out on the final payslip; unused learning budget and wellbeing allowance are not. Company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day, and exit interviews are held by HR rather than by the direct manager.
