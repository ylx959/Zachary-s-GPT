# Tokyo Office Addendum

This addendum adapts the Employee Handbook for employees whose contract is held by Lumen Labs Japan K.K. in Tokyo. Anything not covered here follows the Employee Handbook.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours in Tokyo are 10:00 to 16:00 Japan Standard Time. Because most of the Tokyo team works with colleagues in Taipei and Singapore, meetings with other offices are scheduled between 10:00 and 12:00 whenever possible.

## Annual leave

Annual leave in Japan follows the Labour Standards Act and increases with length of service. Employees receive 10 days in their first year after six months of service, rising by one or two days each year up to a maximum of 20 days. In addition, Lumen Labs Japan grants 5 extra company days per year so that the total is never lower than the global minimum. Japanese law requires every employee to take at least 5 days of leave per year; managers are responsible for making sure this happens.

Unused statutory leave can be carried over for one year, after which it expires.

## Summer and winter holidays

The Tokyo office closes for three days during Obon in mid-August and from December 29 to January 3 for the New Year holidays. These closures replace the global recharge week and do not count against annual leave.

## Remote work

Tokyo employees may work remotely up to two days per week. Mondays and Wednesdays are anchor days in the Tokyo office. Commuting costs are reimbursed in full as a monthly commuter pass, which is the usual practice in Japan; the home office stipend is therefore 300 USD instead of the global amount.

## Expenses

Expenses are claimed in Japanese yen through the same Expensify app as elsewhere. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 8,000 JPY per day. Receipts issued as Japanese qualified invoices must include the issuer's registration number, otherwise the claim is returned.

## Health checks

Japanese law requires an annual health check. Lumen Labs Japan books the health check for every employee at a partner clinic, and the check is done during working hours. Employees aged 35 and above receive an extended check that includes blood tests and an ECG.

## Payroll

Salaries in Japan are paid on the 25th of each month, or on the last business day before the 25th if it falls on a weekend or holiday. Summer and winter bonuses are paid in June and December. Year-end tax adjustment documents must be submitted to HR by November 30.

## Probation and notice

The probation period in Tokyo is six months. After probation, employees must give at least one month of notice when resigning, regardless of whether they are individual contributors or managers.
