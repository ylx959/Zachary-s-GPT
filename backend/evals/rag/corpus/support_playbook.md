# Customer Support Playbook

This playbook tells the support team how to handle customer requests for Atlas robots and the Lumen Home app.

## Support channels and hours

Support is available by chat and email every day from 08:00 to 22:00 Taipei time. Phone support is offered only to customers with an Atlas Care Plus subscription. Social media messages are answered within one business day but should be moved to email as soon as personal data is involved.

## Response time targets

First response targets depend on priority. P1 (device unusable, safety issue): within 1 hour. P2 (major feature broken): within 4 hours. P3 (questions and minor issues): within 24 hours. A ticket that breaches its target is escalated automatically to the team lead.

## Returns and refunds

Customers can return any robot within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as all accessories are included. Opened consumables such as filters and mop pads cannot be returned. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the warehouse receives the return. Customers who bought through a retailer must return the product to that retailer.

## Replacements under warranty

If a defect is confirmed within the warranty period, support ships a replacement unit before the customer sends back the faulty one (advance replacement). The customer then has 14 days to return the faulty unit using the prepaid label; otherwise their card is charged the full price of the replacement.

## Atlas Care Plus

Atlas Care Plus is an optional subscription that costs 9 USD per month. It adds accidental damage cover for one claim per year, phone support, and a free set of filters and brushes every 6 months. Care Plus can be cancelled at any time and stops at the end of the current billing month.

## Shipping

Standard shipping is free and takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs 15 USD and arrives within 2 business days. We currently ship to Taiwan, Japan, Singapore and Australia.

## Escalation

Safety issues, such as a battery that swells, smells of burning, or overheats, must be escalated to the product safety team immediately, regardless of the time of day. Tell the customer to stop using the robot and to unplug the dock. Never ask a customer to open the battery compartment themselves.

## Tone and wording

Use the customer's name, avoid internal jargon such as ticket priorities, and never promise a delivery date before the warehouse has confirmed it. Close every ticket with a short summary of what was done and what happens next.

## Verifying the customer

Before discussing an order or a device, verify the customer with two of the following: the email address on the account, the order number, the last four digits of the serial number, or the postal code of the delivery address. Never ask for a full card number or a password. If the customer cannot be verified, only general information may be shared.

## Common issues and first steps

- Robot does not connect to Wi-Fi: check that the network is 2.4 GHz for the X1, and that the router does not block new devices. Ask the customer to move the robot closer to the router during pairing.
- Map lost or rotated (X2 Pro): ask the customer to delete the map and let the robot remap; maps cannot be restored by support.
- Robot cleans the same area repeatedly (X1): explain that the X1 navigates without a map and that this is expected behaviour, not a defect.
- Short runtime: check how old the battery is and how many charge cycles it has done in the diagnostics page of the admin tool.

## Remote diagnostics

With the customer's consent, support agents can run remote diagnostics from the admin tool. Diagnostics show the battery health, the error history of the last 30 days, the firmware version and the Wi-Fi signal strength. Consent must be recorded in the ticket before diagnostics are started, and diagnostic data is deleted automatically after 90 days.

## Goodwill gestures

Agents may offer a goodwill gesture when the company made a mistake, such as a late delivery or an incorrect answer. Agents can grant a voucher of up to 20 USD on their own. Vouchers between 20 and 100 USD need approval from the team lead. Cash refunds as goodwill are not allowed; use vouchers instead.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

Robots outside the warranty period can be repaired at an authorised service centre. The customer pays a flat diagnostic fee of 25 USD, which is waived if the repair goes ahead. Typical repair costs are shown in the repair price list in the knowledge base. Repairs take about 10 business days including shipping.

## Data protection requests

Requests to delete an account or to export personal data are forwarded to the privacy team through the privacy request form; agents must not delete accounts themselves. The privacy team completes requests within 30 days. When an account is deleted, maps and cleaning history stored in the cloud are deleted as well.

## Quality and feedback

After a ticket is closed, the customer receives a short satisfaction survey. The team target is a satisfaction score of at least 90%. Team leads review five random tickets per agent every month and share feedback in a one-to-one. Recurring problems that generate many tickets are reported to the product team every Friday.

## Holiday season

Between late November and the end of December, ticket volume roughly doubles. Agents may not take annual leave during the two weeks before Christmas unless approved by the support manager. Temporary agents join for the season and handle P3 tickets only.
