# Atlas X3 Mini Robot Vacuum — User Manual

The Atlas X3 Mini is Lumen Labs' compact robot vacuum for studios, dorm rooms and small offices. It is smaller than the Atlas X1 and adds simple room mapping with a ceiling camera.

## Specifications

- Suction power: 3,000 Pa
- Battery: 2,600 mAh lithium-ion
- Runtime: up to 90 minutes on the quiet setting
- Charging time: about 3 hours from empty
- Dustbin capacity: 300 ml
- Water tank: 150 ml clip-on tank (optional accessory)
- Navigation: ceiling camera (vSLAM) plus bump sensors
- Weight: 2.3 kg
- Height: 6.9 cm, the lowest robot in the Atlas range
- Noise level: 56 dB on the quiet setting

## Setting up

Place the compact dock against a wall with at least 0.3 m of free space on each side and 1 m in front of it. Charge the X3 Mini for at least 2 hours before its first run. Pair it with the Lumen Home app by pressing the Home and Power buttons together until the robot says "ready to connect". The X3 Mini supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only.

## Mapping

The ceiling camera builds a simple map during the first two cleaning runs. Because the camera needs light, the X3 Mini maps poorly in dark rooms; schedule the first runs during the day. The app can store one floor map. Virtual walls can be drawn in the app, but no-mop zones are not supported.

## Cleaning modes

The X3 Mini has three suction levels: Eco, Normal and Strong. Edge mode follows walls and furniture around the room once. The optional mopping tank only drips water onto a fixed cloth; the robot cannot lift the cloth, so remove the tank before cleaning carpets.

## Maintenance

Empty the dustbin after every run. Wash the filter every week and replace it every 2 months, because the smaller filter clogs faster. Replace the side brush every 4 months and the main brush every 9 months. Wipe the camera window on top of the robot with a dry cloth once a month.

## Troubleshooting

- Error E1 (wheel stuck): lift the robot and check both drive wheels for hair or debris.
- Error E3 (camera blocked): wipe the camera window and make sure the room is lit.
- Error E5 (cliff sensor dirty): clean the cliff sensors underneath with a dry cloth.
- Error E8 (dustbin missing): insert the dustbin until it clicks.
- If the robot cannot find the dock, make sure the dock has power and is not hidden under furniture.
- To factory reset the X3 Mini, press the reset pinhole under the dustbin lid for 5 seconds with a paperclip.

## Battery care

Keep the robot on its dock between runs. A healthy X3 Mini battery keeps about 80% of its original capacity after 300 full charge cycles. If you store the robot for longer than a month, charge it to around 50% first.

## Warranty

The Atlas X3 Mini comes with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects; its battery is covered for 12 months as well. Students who register the robot with a valid university email address receive an additional 6 months of warranty. Damage caused by liquids other than water in the optional tank is not covered.
