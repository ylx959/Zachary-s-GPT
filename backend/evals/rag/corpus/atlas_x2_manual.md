# Atlas X2 Pro Robot Vacuum and Mop — User Manual

The Atlas X2 Pro is Lumen Labs' flagship robot for larger homes. It vacuums and mops in one run and builds a map of your home.

## Specifications

- Suction power: 6,000 Pa
- Battery: 5,200 mAh lithium-ion
- Runtime: up to 180 minutes on the quiet setting
- Charging time: about 5.5 hours from empty
- Dustbin capacity: 400 ml (emptied automatically by the dock)
- Water tank: 300 ml
- Navigation: LiDAR laser mapping plus front obstacle camera
- Weight: 3.8 kg
- Height: 9.7 cm including the LiDAR turret
- Noise level: 55 dB on the quiet setting

## Setting up

Place the self-emptying dock against a wall with at least 0.5 m of free space on each side and 1.5 m in front of it. Fill the water tank before the first mopping run. Pair the X2 Pro with the Lumen Home app by holding the Power button for 5 seconds until the light pulses white. The X2 Pro supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

## Mapping and no-go zones

On its first run the X2 Pro builds a map in about 15 minutes for a 100 square metre home. The app can store up to 4 floor maps. You can draw no-go zones and no-mop zones in the app, for example around a pet bowl or over a carpet. The robot automatically lifts its mop pad by 7 mm when it detects a carpet.

## Self-emptying dock

The dock empties the robot's dustbin after every run into a 2.5 litre disposable bag. A full bag holds roughly 60 days of debris. The dock light turns orange when the bag needs replacing.

## Maintenance

Wash the mop pad after every mopping run, or let the dock dry it with warm air. Wash the filter every two weeks and replace it every 6 months. Replace the side brush every 6 months and the main roller brush every 12 months. Descale the water tank once a month if you live in an area with hard water.

## Troubleshooting

- Error E1 (wheel stuck): lift the robot and check both drive wheels for hair or debris.
- Error E7 (LiDAR blocked): check that nothing is pressing on the LiDAR turret and that it can spin freely.
- Error E9 (water tank empty): refill the tank or switch to vacuum-only mode in the app.
- If the map looks rotated or duplicated, delete it in the app and let the robot remap.
- To factory reset the X2 Pro, hold the Power and Home buttons together for 15 seconds.

## Warranty

The Atlas X2 Pro comes with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, and the battery is covered for 18 months. Registering the robot in the Lumen Home app within 30 days of purchase extends the warranty by one extra year.

## Package contents

The Atlas X2 Pro box contains the robot, the self-emptying dock with one dust bag pre-installed, two spare dust bags, two washable mop pads, a 300 ml cleaning solution, one spare filter, one spare side brush, the power cable and a quick start guide.

## Indicator lights

- Solid white ring: ready and fully charged.
- Rotating white ring: mapping or cleaning in progress.
- Pulsing white: charging on the dock.
- Blinking blue ring: pairing mode.
- Blinking red ring: error; see the app for details.
- Solid orange on the dock: the dust bag is full or missing.

## Cleaning modes and suction

The X2 Pro offers four suction levels: Quiet, Balanced, Turbo and Max. Carpet boost raises suction to Max automatically on carpets and returns to the selected level on hard floors. Mopping has three water flow levels: Low, Medium and High. Room-by-room cleaning lets you choose a different suction level and water flow for every room, as well as the cleaning order.

## Obstacle avoidance

The front camera recognises common obstacles such as cables, socks, shoes and pet waste and keeps a safety distance of about 5 cm around them. Images used for obstacle recognition are processed on the robot and are not uploaded unless you enable "Share obstacle photos" in the app to help improve recognition. The camera can be switched off completely in the privacy settings; the robot then relies on LiDAR and bump sensors only.

## Multi-floor homes

The X2 Pro detects automatically which floor it is on when you carry it to a different level, as long as that floor has already been mapped. Each floor map has its own no-go zones and schedules. If you move the dock to a different floor, the robot needs about two minutes to relocate itself.

## Battery care

Keep the robot on the dock when it is not in use. The X2 Pro supports "resume after charging": if the battery runs low during a large job, it returns to the dock, charges to 80%, and continues where it stopped. A healthy X2 Pro battery keeps about 80% of its original capacity after 600 full charge cycles.

## Replacement parts

Dust bags are sold in packs of six for 19 USD. A maintenance kit for the X2 Pro with two filters, two side brushes, one roller brush and four mop pads costs 49 USD. Replacing the battery yourself is not recommended; battery replacements are handled by authorised service centres.

## Safety instructions

Do not use the X2 Pro outdoors or on wet floors. Only fill the water tank with clean tap water or with the official cleaning solution; other detergents can damage the pump. Do not let the robot mop thick carpets with the mop pad attached if carpet detection has been switched off. Only use the original power cable supplied with the dock.

## Technical support

If the troubleshooting steps above do not solve the problem, contact support through the Lumen Home app. The serial number is printed under the dustbin and starts with "X2P-". Firmware updates for the X2 Pro are offered in the app and installed only after you confirm them.
